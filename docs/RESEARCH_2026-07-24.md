## Highest-value findings

### Implement now: reviewer-facing tamper evidence

AuditWeave (arXiv:2607.09682, July 2026) describes a dependency-free SHA-256 hash chain for reviewer-navigable evidence. Its reported evaluation detected edits, insertions, deletions, and reordering across 2,000 mutation trials. The paper explicitly limits the claim to tamper evidence, not prevention, and notes that an actor controlling storage could recompute a chain unless its head is externally anchored.

Transparency.dev and Trillian reinforce the product value of verifiable histories for financial records and auditor review. LedgerMatch therefore adds a separate human-review ledger whose events bind the original reconciliation `run_id`, reviewer, timestamp, transaction, decision, selected invoices, reason, predecessor hash, and event hash.

### Evaluate later: probabilistic candidate discovery

Splink 4 demonstrates probabilistic one-to-one linkage of financial transactions when dates drift, fees alter amounts, and memos are truncated. It is useful for generating candidates, but its training assumptions and probabilities must not become authoritative accounting decisions. A future optional adapter may emit review candidates while LedgerMatch retains exact conservation and human approval gates.

### Benchmark later: accelerated many-to-many search

`europeanplaice/subset_sum` (`dpss`) is an active MIT-licensed Rust/Python reconciliation engine with bounded group size, tolerance, and multiple candidates. It is suitable for a benchmark adapter, not an immediate runtime dependency. LedgerMatch must first establish parity on ambiguity enumeration, deterministic ordering, limits, and exact-conservation policy.

### Keep as release tooling, not product runtime

- Playwright: browser regression tests.
- Semgrep and CodeQL: static-analysis gates.
- Gitleaks: secret scanning.
- k6/Artillery: hosted load testing.
- OpenTelemetry/Sentry: future opt-in operational telemetry with financial-field redaction.
- OCR projects such as olmOCR and Unlimited-OCR: defer until practitioner evidence shows structured exports are insufficient.
- Agent frameworks: use for development orchestration only; never authorize financial allocations.

## Product decision

The defensible wedge is not another opaque matching score. It is proof-carrying reconciliation plus a review trail: deterministic proposals, preserved ambiguity, independent bundle validation, human adjudication, and tamper-evident review history. This improves trust and handoff value without making unsupported automation or compliance claims.

## Non-claims and kill rules

- A hash chain detects alteration relative to its current head; it does not prevent rewriting by an actor who controls both storage and the head.
- External anchoring is deferred and must be separately threat-modeled.
- Probabilistic linkage remains candidate-only until calibrated on representative labeled data.
- Stop expanding solver sophistication if practitioner shadow runs do not show recurring grouped ambiguity or review-time improvement.
