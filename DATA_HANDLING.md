# Data handling

- Prefer de-identified or synthetic inputs.
- Store generated reports only in operator-approved locations.
- Apply least privilege and an appropriate retention schedule.
- Treat evidence bundles and payment-import files as financial records.
- Never commit inputs, outputs, keys, or local environment files.
- Verify destruction and backup duties under applicable operator and user jurisdictions.

Draft template - not legal advice - requires qualified counsel review before operative use.
